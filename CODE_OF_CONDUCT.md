# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The full text is at that link; the short version is below and
does not replace it.

## The short version

Be civil. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code,
the measurement or the argument — not the person making it.

Not acceptable: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks,
sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information,
sustained disruption.

Technical disagreement is welcome and expected. "This benchmark does not
support that conclusion" is on topic; it is the kind of comment this
project has benefited from most.

## Scope

Applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@vantino.ch**. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainers will respond as promptly as a small team can, and may warn,
remove content, or ban for repeated or serious violations.

The maintainers are subject to this document as well. A project with one
active author has no independent appeal path, which is worth stating
plainly rather than implying otherwise.
